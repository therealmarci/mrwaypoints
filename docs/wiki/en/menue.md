# Waypoint menu & guide

**`/waypoints`** (also `/waypoint`, `/wegpunkte`, `/wegpunkt`, `/wps`) opens a small menu. Everything in it applies to the **world or dimension you are in right now** - you see waypoints in the Nether while in the Nether.

## The start menu
| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **My waypoints** | your own waypoints here |
| **Create waypoint** | a dialog with name and category - only categories you may create in right now (with "used/limit"). The waypoint is placed at your position. Gray if you may not create anywhere. |
| **All waypoints** | all server waypoints here |
| Middle: **guide** | only while one is running - a click ends it |
| bottom: **Categories** · **Settings** · **Close** | **Categories** (only with the mod): which categories appear in Xaero; **Settings**: view distance and display in Xaero (see [In Xaero's Minimap](xaero.md)) |

## In the lists
| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Click** on a waypoint | Guide in the action bar: arrow relative to where you look, distance, ▲/▼ height difference. Ends on arrival, with `/waypoints stop` or at the top of the start menu. |
| **Right click** on your own waypoint | Edit: rename, move to your own position, delete |
| bottom **Category** · **Sorting** · **Search** | Filters: a click switches (category: next, sorting: distance → name → newest), search via a dialog - **right click resets the filter**. The top middle shows what is filtered right now. |

## Good to know
- The team decides in which categories you may create waypoints and how many. When the limit is reached, the button is gray.
- You may always rename, move and delete your own waypoints.
- **Private waypoints** you create as usual directly in Xaero's Minimap - they stay on your PC. Server waypoints only exist on the server.
- With MRadmin you also find the waypoints on the **map** of your player page (`/web`) and create new ones there with a right click.
